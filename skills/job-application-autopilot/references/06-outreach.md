# Recruiter and hiring-manager outreach

## Why

A short, specific message to a recruiter or hiring manager for a role the candidate has just applied to often gets more replies than dozens of cold applications. It works best for top-choice roles and for companies that clearly sponsor visas.

## Who to contact

- The recruiter named on the posting, or the "posted by" person on LinkedIn.
- The hiring manager or engineering manager for the team (search the company on LinkedIn by team or title).
- Agencies that specialise in the target country and in international candidates.

Contact one or two people per role, not the whole company.

## The approval rule

Everything sent in the candidate's name goes through them:
1. Write the batch: email drafts saved in the candidate's mailbox (unsent), LinkedIn messages typed but not sent, or the text in chat.
2. Show the batch to the candidate.
3. Send only the ones they approve, exactly as approved. Approval for one message does not cover others.
4. Log each sent message in the application log notes ("outreach sent to <name>, <date>").

Send from the email address on the CV, so replies reach the inbox the candidate monitors. If the candidate prefers to attach the CV and press send themselves, leave drafts and tell them which ones are ready.

## Writing the message

Follow the candidate's writing-style rules from answers.md. Defaults that work:
- Under 120 words for email, under 300 characters for a LinkedIn connection note.
- One line on who they are and the exact role. One concrete matching achievement with a number. One plain line on the visa or relocation situation ("I am based in <city> and would need visa sponsorship, I handle relocation myself"). A simple ask ("Happy to share more or jump on a short call").
- No flattery, no buzzwords, no long CV recap. Plain sentences read as human.
- Subject line for email: "<Role> application, <Name>".

Example (adapt, do not copy verbatim):

> Hi <first name>, I just applied for the <role> role at <company>. I have <n> years building <relevant thing>, most recently <one achievement with a number>. I am based in <city> and would need visa sponsorship, relocation I can handle myself. Happy to share more or have a short call if it helps. Thanks, <name>

## Follow-ups

One polite follow-up after about a week with no reply is fine. After that, stop. Never message the same person about several roles at once.

## Replies

When someone replies, add it to action-items.md immediately. Interview invitations and scheduling links are the highest priority items in the whole pipeline.
