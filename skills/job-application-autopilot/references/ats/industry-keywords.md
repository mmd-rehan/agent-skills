## Notes for Usage

- **Match first** — only pull from these banks if the word/phrase also appears (or closely appears) in the JD
- **Don't pad** — adding keywords the candidate doesn't actually have is dishonest and will fail interview screening
- **Variant forms** — include both full form and abbreviation for any term listed here in the Skills section
- **Prioritize JD language** — these banks are supplements; the JD is the primary keyword source
