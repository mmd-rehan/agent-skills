# Bullet Writing Bank & Transformation Guide

Use this when candidates struggle to articulate their achievements or provide only vague duty descriptions. Transform weak bullets into ATS-optimized, metric-rich accomplishment statements.

---

## The Transformation Method

**Step 1 — Ask the Consulting Questions**
For any vague bullet, ask:
- "How many of X did you handle?" (volume)
- "How long did this take before vs. after?" (time improvement)
- "What was the business impact?" (revenue, cost, risk, satisfaction)
- "How big was the team / system / budget?" (scale)
- "Was this a first-time implementation?" (novelty = differentiation)

**Step 2 — Apply the Formula**
`[Strong Verb] + [What You Did] + [How/Tool Used] + [Result/Impact]`

**Step 3 — Add a Metric**
If no hard number exists, use:
- Qualifiers: "significant," "measurable," "consistent"
- Relative: "reduced by over 30%," "from 4 hours to under 20 minutes"
- Scale: "across 500+ endpoints," "for a team of 12"

## When There Are NO Metrics

Use these strategies when the candidate genuinely has no numbers:

### Strategy 1 — Use Scope/Scale
- "across a 500-user organization"
- "for a 3-country operation"
- "supporting 20+ business units"

### Strategy 2 — Use Frequency/Volume
- "managing 50+ daily alerts"
- "processing 200+ tickets per month"
- "overseeing weekly deployments"

### Strategy 3 — Use Before/After Language
- "reducing a previously manual process to an automated workflow"
- "eliminating recurring incidents that previously required 2+ hours of manual investigation"

### Strategy 4 — Use Superlatives Carefully
- "first implementation of X in the organization"
- "primary owner of the most business-critical integration"
- "sole developer responsible for..."

### Strategy 5 — Acknowledge and Rephrase
If you truly cannot quantify, shift to specificity:
- ❌ "Improved performance" (too vague)
- ✅ "Improved alert response time by optimizing ServiceNow Business Rule execution order and removing redundant queries" (specific even without a number)

## Consulting Prompts (ask the user)

If bullets are vague, ask these questions:

1. "What happened because of your work that wouldn't have happened without you?"
2. "How many people / systems / transactions did this affect?"
3. "Did this save time? By how much — a few hours a week? Days?"
4. "Did leadership recognize this? What was the feedback?"
5. "Was this a new solution or an improvement on an existing one?"
6. "How does your employer / client benefit financially from this?"
